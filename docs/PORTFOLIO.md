# Portfolio Presentation

**GitHub repository title:** Azula WooCommerce Inventory Manager & Stock History

**Suggested LinkedIn Featured title:** WooCommerce Inventory Management & SKU Stock History

**Suggested description:**

A custom WooCommerce inventory workspace built around real e-commerce operations. The project began as an audit trail for SKU stock movements after discrepancies between WooCommerce quantities and warehouse availability. It grew into a consolidated inventory interface to check products and stock status, edit quantities, filter and export records, inspect SKU movement history and review/correct missing or inaccurate categories through explicit manager actions. Business workflow design, debugging, validation planning and implementation were developed iteratively with AI assistance.

## Before and after (qualitative, not measured)

**Before:** staff had to navigate multiple WooCommerce Products and variation editor screens for common stock checks, corrections and category reviews.

**After:** three tabs inside one Inventory workspace provide SKU-centric lookup and quantity updates, source-attributed stock history for supported operations, and review-driven category management.
